Earnings per activity

Coins are credited to the acting user's earnings balance for each successful like, comment, share, and first watch of a video. A user can earn the watch reward once per video.

Promotion price per unit

Set the coin cost per requested like, follower, video view, and profile view. Paid campaigns enter a pending queue for manual fulfillment; purchases do not fabricate engagement counts.
