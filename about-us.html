About EarnTok

A place to create, connect, and grow.

EarnTok is a social platform built around short-form video, community, and creator discovery. We bring people together to share ideas, find new voices, and take part in a lively digital community.

Who we are

A social space for people and ideas.

EarnTok is designed to make discovering and sharing video feel approachable. Whether you are posting your first clip, following a creator whose perspective you enjoy, or catching up with friends, the platform gives you ways to participate at your own pace.

Our community is shaped by its members. Creators bring their skills, humor, knowledge, culture, and everyday experiences; viewers bring curiosity, encouragement, conversation, and new points of view. We want those interactions to make EarnTok more useful, welcoming, and interesting over time.

We build for people using a range of devices, connections, and levels of experience. Clear controls, understandable product information, and reliable account and payment experiences are important parts of that work.

Discover videos

Explore short videos and creator profiles, and follow the people and topics you want to see more of.

Create and share

Use coin features

Our purpose

Help creativity find its people.

We believe creative expression should be easy to share and rewarding to discover. EarnTok aims to give creators straightforward ways to publish, present their profiles, and reach an audience, while giving viewers an inviting way to find fresh content and participate.

Growth takes time and depends on many things, including what someone shares, who finds it, and how the community responds. We do not promise a particular audience size, income, or outcome. Our focus is to offer useful tools and keep improving the experience around them.

Our principles

The standards we work toward.

Safety and respect

We want members to treat one another with care. Use account controls thoughtfully, protect private information, and contact support when something about your experience needs attention.

Clarity and transparency

We aim to explain how features work and show relevant amounts, rates, and request status before you take action. Read the details on each screen and keep your receipts and references.

Access and usability

We work to make core tasks understandable across screen sizes and use clear labels, feedback, and navigation to support a broad range of members and devices.

Coins and payments

Understand the details before you pay.

Coin purchases, withdrawals, and payment options depend on the features and methods currently available in your account. Rates, currencies, minimums, maximums, and processing requirements can differ by method or merchant. Review the current on-screen details carefully before submitting a payment or withdrawal request.

A request may need review before it is completed. Keep your transaction reference and proof of payment, never send funds to an address or account that does not match the instructions shown to you, and contact support if a request appears incorrect or delayed.

Helpful account practices

  • Use a strong password and keep your sign-in details private.
  • Confirm currency, rate, amount, and recipient before sending payment.
  • Keep transaction receipts and refer to your purchase or withdrawal history.
  • Use in-app support for account or transaction questions.
